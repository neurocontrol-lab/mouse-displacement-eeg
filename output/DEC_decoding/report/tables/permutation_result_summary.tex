Eight-channel shrinkage LDA exceeded the within-recording label-shuffle null after Holm correction in 23/23 non-pilot participants. The unadjusted participant-specific 95th-percentile chance thresholds ranged from 52.6\% to 54.9\%. These thresholds describe the null distribution; adjusted significance is determined by the Holm-corrected $p$-values, not by crossing the unadjusted threshold alone.
Raw primary p-values ranged from 0.0005 to 0.0065; 22/23 participants reached the Monte Carlo minimum of 0.0005. All primary Holm-adjusted p-values were 0.0115. Repeated adjusted values reflect the step-down correction and finite sampling resolution, not identical effect sizes. Values at the Monte Carlo floor do not resolve smaller exact tail probabilities.
