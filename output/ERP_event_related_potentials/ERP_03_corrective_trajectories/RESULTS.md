# ERP_03 — Corrective versus erroneous trajectories

The jump-aligned positivity was present in both manuscript trajectory classes. At Cz/FCz, the non-pilot averages peaked at **278 ms (+3.87 µV)** for corrective reaches and **284 ms (+4.04 µV)** for erroneous reaches, using the largest positive sample from 200 to 400 ms after the jump. These timings describe the cohort averages.

## Trials and matching

Of 10,155 EEG-accepted displaced trials, the manuscript labelled 4,427 corrective, 5,306 erroneous, 421 slow and one unknown. Matching without replacement within recording and jump direction yielded **2,401 corrective–erroneous pairs** from 322 recordings and all 25 participants. The non-pilot cohort contributed 2,256 pairs and the two pilots contributed 145. The median absolute gap within pairs was 10 ms for reaction time and 8 ms for movement-to-jump delay. The corresponding 95th percentiles were 100 and 44 ms.

The manuscript labels combine lateral velocity and endpoint distributions. On the independent AUD_02 check, 232 matched erroneous reaches had steering confirmed by 250 ms and 1,436 were confirmed later. This illustrates the overlap between the two behavioural definitions. Geometric hit/miss requires a verified target boundary and scoring rule.
